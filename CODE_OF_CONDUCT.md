# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

In the interest of fostering an open, respectful, and productive environment, contributors and maintainers of this repository are expected to interact professionally and constructively.

Participation in this project should be free of harassment, hostility, and discriminatory behavior.

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- being respectful in written and technical discussion,
- focusing criticism on ideas, implementations, and evidence rather than people,
- welcoming constructive feedback,
- acknowledging uncertainty and methodological limits where appropriate,
- communicating clearly when changes affect reproducibility or scientific interpretation.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- personal attacks,
- harassment or intimidation,
- abusive or insulting language,
- dismissive or hostile responses to questions,
- knowingly misrepresenting technical or scientific behavior of the code.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and related project communication channels.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, or contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

---
